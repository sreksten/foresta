// The shopkeepers' greetings when the party enters a shop for the first time (shop entrance intermezzi). The first alternative of
// each is the original greeting: it says what the shop does, the others play around it.
SALUTO_ARMAIOLO
    Welcome to my armourer's shop. I buy and sell weapons, armour, helmets, everything of the finest quality.
    Come in, come in! Here we forge, buy and sell: weapons, armour, helmets. No junk, only the good stuff.
    Welcome. Swords, shields, armour and helmets: if it hits things or stops things hitting you, you'll find it here.
    Step inside, but don't touch the merchandise with dirty gloves. I buy and sell every kind of weapon and armour.

SALUTO_ALCHIMISTA
    Welcome to my alchemist's shop! I can sell you spells, potions, maps, or increase your magic power.
    Come in, come in, but don't touch anything! I have spells, potions and maps, and I can also increase your magic power.
    Welcome to my shop! Potions, spells, maps and, for those who want more, a nice magical upgrade.
    Ah, customers! Mind where you step: some potions are still being tested. I sell spells, potions and maps, and I increase magic power.

SALUTO_INCANTATORE
    Welcome to my enchantment workshop. I can improve your weapons with that extra pinch of magic.
    Welcome to my workshop. Hand me your weapons and I'll give them back with that extra pinch of magic.
    Hello there! Enchantments are the house specialty: I can improve your weapons with a touch of magic.

SALUTO_VENDITORE
    Welcome to my shop of Scrolls, Amulets, Knick-knacks and Trinkets! These are the basic ingredients needed to improve your weapons.
    Welcome! Scrolls, amulets, knick-knacks and trinkets: everything you need to improve your weapons, right at hand.
    Step right up! Trinkets and knick-knacks of every sort, plus the basic ingredients to upgrade your weapons.
