SCENA_CITTA
	In the alleys of the city the party asks around, and someone remembers./Someone here must have seen something./Let's ask at the shop too.
	The party stops in the square to watch the comings and goings: somewhere there's a clue./Let's look closely, nobody is entirely invisible./I can hear the gossip.

SCENA_LOCANDA
	The fireplace crackles and a dog sleeps under a table. The party takes a seat and waits for someone to step forward./The nice thing about inns is that nobody stays quiet for long./I'll keep an eye on the door.
	The common room of the inn is full of smoke and voices. The party sits down and listens closely./An inn always knows everything./I'll buy the first round, if needed.
	The innkeeper polishes a glass and looks at the party with the face of someone who has a story to sell./How much does the story cost?/Not much, usually.

SCENA_ROVINE
	The party moves among the fallen walls. The wind makes noises that sound like words./Look at the ground, someone has passed through./There's something behind that column.
	Grass grows among the stones, and under the grass are marks that someone removed in a hurry./They were looking for something here, and didn't find it./Or they found it, and wiped out the tracks.
	The sun filters through a fallen arch and lights up a wall covered in graffiti. The party stops to look./Someone has written recently./Let's find the meaning, and we'll find the way.

SCENA_TEMPIO
	In the silence of the temple every step sounds like a question. The party looks around with respect./Let's search with care./Let's not touch anything without asking.
	The light from the stained glass falls on the altar in coloured patches. The party stops, and for a moment nobody speaks./There's something here that wants to be found./Or that wants to be left in peace.
	A candle is still lit at one side of the altar. The party approaches on tiptoe./Someone passed through recently./And left something behind.

SCENA_GROTTA
	The air in the cave smells of wet stone. The party lights a torch and proceeds slowly./Watch where you put your feet./I hear the sound of water, and maybe something more.
	The walls of the cave throw back every word with a mocking echo. The party lowers its voice./Whoever comes here does so for a reason./Or because they lost their way.
	A narrow tunnel leads to a larger chamber, where someone lit a fire and then put it out./Ash still warm./We're close.

SCENA_BOSCO
	The branches above the path form a green tunnel, and the party advances looking at the trunks./Let's look for marks on the bark./Someone passed this way, the grass is bent.
	A woodpecker knocks in the distance, and between one tap and the next the woods seem to hold their breath./Let's stop and look carefully./I don't trust a forest this quiet.
	The path splits in two and the party stops to study the ground./Footprints, and fresh ones./That way.

SCENA_PALUDE
	Mist rises from the water and every step sinks into the mud. The party walks in single file, eyes on the ground./Don't be fooled by the lights./I prefer dry land.
	Clouds of insects buzz among the reeds, and a heron looks at the party with an air of superiority./It's watching us./Let's see what it's hiding.
	A walkway of rotten planks crosses a stretch of stagnant water. The party lines up to cross one at a time./Light weights, please./I already have one wet foot.

SCENA_RADURA
	The clearing is a circle of tall grass, and the wind makes it sway like a green sea. The party stops in the middle./It's nice here./That's why it's worth looking more carefully.
	A stream crosses the clearing and sings among the stones. The party stops to listen./Someone filled a canteen here./And passed through recently.
	The grass is flattened in a circle, as where someone lay down to look at the sky./A perfect place to think./A perfect place to leave a message.
